Lenses#

Basler's lens portfolio includes fixed focal lenses with all common lens mount types, focal lengths, and iris ranges as well as telecentric lenses.

Basler offers their own range of lenses as well as partner lenses by other leading manufacturers. While all lenses are tested to ensure full compatibility with Basler cameras, the Basler Lenses have been designed specifically for Basler cameras and are manufactured exclusively for Basler.

To find the right lens for your application, visit the Basler website.

Also included in the portfolio are lens accessories, like mount adapters and spacer rings, which let you securely mount a lens on a camera.
