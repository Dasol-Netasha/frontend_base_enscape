Datasheet

Domino Melody

Low-profile frame grabber for one single-tap non-standard analog camera


  • One 10-bit 40 MHz A/D converter with one 8- or 10-bit LUT
  • 32-bit 33 MHz PCI bus, standard and low profile brackets
  • D³ technology for extremely low synchronization jitter
  • Support for mega-pixel progressive-scan and interlaced cameras, asynchronous reset and exposure control
  • Trigger, strobe and general purpose I/O lines

Number of lines

4 I/O lines:

1 differential input (TR)

Connectors

SYSTEM: 8-pin RJ-45 connector on the bracket

SYSTEM INTERNAL: 10-pin dual-row 0.1" shrouded header on the PCB

CAMERA POWER: 4-pin Molex connector on the PCB for camera powering

Usage

Camera and image acquisition trigger

Light strobe trigger

General purpose IO

Electrical specifications

TR: Inputs compatible with TTL, LVTTL, differential LVDS, differential RS-422, differential RS-485 signals, protected against electrostatic discharge, with choke for the rejection of common-mode noise

ST: Opto-isolated outputs, compatible with signals up to 60V, with 3 µs fall time, isolated up to 500V

ENHANCED IO Inputs or outputs compatible with TTL signals, protected against electrostatic discharge, with high-frequency noise filter
